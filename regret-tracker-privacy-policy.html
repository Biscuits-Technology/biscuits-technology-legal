Privacy Policy — Regret Tracker

Last updated: 17 June 2026

Regret Tracker (“the app”) is an Android app published by Biscuits Technology (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: your purchase data never leaves your device, and we do not collect or have access to it.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter — the purchases you log, their prices, dates, photos, categories, your check-in answers, your hourly-pay setting, themes, and progress (XP, achievements) — is stored only on your device using the app’s local storage. There is no account, no login, and no server. We cannot see this data, and it is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Photos

If you attach a photo to a purchase, the app references that image from your device’s storage. Photos stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Backups

The app lets you export a backup file and restore from one. These files are created and read only when you choose to, and are saved to the location you pick on your device. We never receive them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process certain information, including your device’s advertising identifier and standard technical/usage data, in accordance with Google’s policies. We do not receive personally identifying information from this. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device’s Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

Regret Tracker is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your control & deleting your data

You can delete all app data at any time from Settings → Wipe everything, or by uninstalling the app. Because data is local to your device, this permanently removes it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
