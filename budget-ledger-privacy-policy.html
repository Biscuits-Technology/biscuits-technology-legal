Privacy Policy

Budget Ledger — Biscuits Technology LLC — Effective: May 20, 2026

Short version: Budget Ledger stores everything on your device. We do not have servers. We do not collect your financial data. We do not sell anything about you to anyone.

1. Who We Are

Budget Ledger is developed and published by Biscuits Technology LLC. This policy applies to the Budget Ledger mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or have any access to the following:

  • Your transactions, account names, balances, or recurring schedules
  • Your financial history
  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Your location
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking

Budget Ledger does not require an account, login, or internet connection to function.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

All app data — transactions, categories, accounts, recurring transactions, your app-lock PIN, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using the operating system's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to back it up.

4. Backup & Restore (File Access)

Budget Ledger lets you save and restore your data as a file. When you tap "Save Backup File," the app asks you to choose a folder and writes a single backup file (a .json file) to that location. When you tap "Restore from File," you select a backup file and the app reads it back in.

The app accesses your device's file storage only during these actions that you start yourself, and only the specific folder or file you choose. We never browse, scan, or upload your files, and the backup file stays wherever you put it.

5. Notifications

Budget Ledger can send you a low-balance alert — a notification when an account's balance drops below a threshold you set. These notifications are generated entirely on your device; nothing is transmitted to us or anyone else. The app requests notification permission only for this feature, and you can revoke it at any time in your device settings.

6. App Lock (PIN & Biometrics)

You can optionally protect the app with a PIN and with fingerprint or face unlock. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system — the app never sees or stores your fingerprint or face data. Your PIN is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted.

7. Support & Bug Reports

If you contact us through the in-app support or bug-report feature, the app opens your device's native email application with a message pre-filled. That message may include the app version, the screen you were on, and a short list of recent in-app action names (such as "opened settings") to help us diagnose problems — it does not include your transactions, balances, or any financial data. We receive only the message you choose to send and use it solely to respond to you.

Support contact: info@biscuitstechnology.com

8. Advertising

Budget Ledger displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. As part of serving ads, Google may collect device identifiers and usage signals in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We have no access to this data and do not sell user data to advertisers directly.

Ads are displayed as non-intrusive banners at the bottom of the screen only. We do not display full-screen or interstitial ads.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use its ad services, visit: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

9. Third-Party Services

Budget Ledger uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — banner advertising (see Section 8)
  • Expo / React Native — app framework, operates on-device only

No third-party analytics, crash reporting, or user tracking SDKs are included.

10. Children's Privacy

Budget Ledger is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

11. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Using the Clear All Data option in Settings to erase all transactions, accounts, and settings
  • Uninstalling the app, which removes all locally stored data

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
